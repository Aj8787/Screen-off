Off Time

Privacy policy

Off Time is an Android app developed by Saiyed Adil. It calculates screen-off time and longest breaks locally on your device.

Effective date: 11 September 2026
Android package: io.github.aj8787.offtime

Information used on your device

With your permission, Off Time uses Android Usage Access to read screen-state timestamps. It uses those timestamps to calculate today's screen-off total and longest break. Usage Access can expose broader device activity; Off Time only uses the screen-state information needed for these calculations.

Your selected sleep schedule, appearance and widget settings are stored in the app's private storage. Sleep exclusion uses the schedule you enter; it does not detect whether you are sleeping.

No off-device collection or sharing

Off Time does not send activity or settings to the developer or a server, and does not sell or share personal data. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or internet permission. No cloud service is used for tracking. Android and your device manufacturer manage their own system usage history.

Permissions and background operation

Usage Access is optional to grant, but necessary to calculate totals. On supported versions of Android, notification permission can be granted for the optional ongoing summary.

A foreground service may run while a home-screen widget is placed or the summary is enabled, to receive screen-state events and refresh the displays. Android controls the foreground-service notification. A boot/package-update receiver restores eligible widget scheduling after a restart or update; Android restrictions may require reopening the app.

Retention and your choices

Saved settings remain until you change them, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android controls retention of its underlying usage history. You can revoke Usage Access or notifications in Android Settings at any time.

Remove the widgets and disable the summary to stop their foreground service. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's saved local settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies this version.
